# Example: `/steve cut ROADMAP.md`

**The ask.** Cut a Q3 roadmap that is too big for the team that has to build it.

**Input:** a 12-item Q3 roadmap for a 2-person invoicing startup with 900 paying users

---

**Mode:** cut · Ledgerly Q3 roadmap

**The one thing.** "Ledgerly gets freelancers paid faster, with less work."

Twelve items, two people, thirteen weeks. "Every item has someone asking for it" is not a reason to keep them — someone asks for everything. That sentence is the disease, not the diagnosis.

**Stays (3).**
1. **Recurring invoices** (#2) — the same invoice, every month, is what a freelancer's year actually looks like. The only item on this page that removes work instead of adding a screen.
2. **Stripe** — one of the six in #8. "Paid" is the verb in your one sentence. A pay button inside the invoice is the shortest line between the work and the money.
3. **Chasing.** Not on your list, which is the most interesting thing about your list. Nothing here makes a late client pay. Polite automatic reminders at day 3, 14, 30. It is the only thing you can ship this quarter that puts money in a user's bank account.

**Goes.**
- ~~Mobile app, iOS + Android~~ — two people, three codebases. Nobody writes an invoice on a bus. → you get back the quarter.
- ~~Client portal with logins~~ — a password is a wall between you and your money. A link that needs no account does the same job. → no signup flow, no resets, no support queue behind them.
- ~~Expense tracking with OCR~~ and ~~time tracking with a desktop app~~ — two other products with two other names. → you stop fighting Expensify and Toggl with one engineer each.
- ~~PayPal, Wise, QuickBooks, Xero, Zapier~~ — five APIs that break on someone else's schedule. → weeks back, and one integration you can actually make good.
- ~~AI invoice descriptions~~ — nobody ever paid late because the wording was flat. → your credibility for the day you ship AI that matters.
- ~~Team accounts~~ — your customer is one person. The accountant wants a CSV in January, not a seat all year.
- ~~Public API + webhooks~~ — 900 users is not a platform, and an API is the one promise you can never take back.
- ~~White-label for agencies~~ — that is a different company with a different name. → you keep being one product.

**Folds into a default.**
- Dark mode → follow the operating system. An afternoon, not a roadmap line.
- Multi-currency → not a feature, a field: the client's currency on the invoice, that day's rate, no settings. And this one is taste, not a measurement — if your data says a real share of invoices already cross a border, it takes the third slot and chasing waits.

**How to say no.** "We're not building it this quarter. Everything we ship before October is about getting your invoices paid faster — that's it. I've written yours down and I'll come back to you if that changes." Keep a `NO.md` in the repo with what you turned down and why, so you only have this argument once.

**What Steve would say.** *(imagined)* "That's not a roadmap. That's a list of twelve people you're afraid to disappoint."

> "Focusing is about saying no. Focusing is about saying no. And you've got to say 'no, no, no'." (WWDC closing session, 1997)
